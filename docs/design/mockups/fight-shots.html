Shots in a fight on the map (#339)

The same fight scene as in orders-style.html (scene 4), in the style chosen by the owner: the arteriya map, BL1 shading with border highlight, texture T2, haze F1, roads R1, arena AR1, effects based on FX3 "Expressive", strength medium. Only the shots themselves change here: five variants and the current one. The fight engine plays DEC-24 and DEC-32 round by round (a round of 2 s, one shot from each side, bonus ones first), so the picture shows what the server would say. The sides differ by color and by shape (the enemy has a double outline), a real shot is a square, a bonus one is a hollow diamond.

How the fight states differ (the same in all variants, the details of each in the cards)
real shot: a solid square ■ in the color of the side, spends a charge
bonus shot: a hollow diamond ◇, a paler body, the caption "◇ bonus attack"
cancellation: two shots meet in the middle, a collision and a cross, nobody is hit
unit kill: ▽ shards in the color of the loser and "−N"
silent charge (plains, DEC-24, F2): an amber sign on the group, a dim dashed ring on the round tick, no projectile, no flash
bounce: a dashed arrow back; capture: a wave and a flag (as in FX3)

A round of 2 s: does the animation fit

The round tick is zero. A shot that kills flies for 0.6 s; two cancelling ones meet after 0.3 s (the speed is the same). The 0.35 s wind-up runs before the tick, so the next round starts its wind-up 0.35 s before its own tick.

The frame price: three fights at once

This is not a measurement: the table is computed on the page by a dry run of the "three fights" scene, from what the same frame would put on the PixiJS side (a Graphics primitive is redrawn every frame, a sprite from a pool with a baked texture, a `ParticleContainer` particle, text from a pool; no filters). Weights: Graphics 1, sprite 0.5, text 2, particle 0.05. The estimate relative to flat drawing (section 24.4 of ui-style: 1.00) is tied to FX3 from #339 (the worst frame 1.10-1.25, taken 1.18; the average about 1.03) and scaled by the weight. Before implementation a PixiJS prototype and a measurement on integrated graphics are needed (software WebGL distorts).
