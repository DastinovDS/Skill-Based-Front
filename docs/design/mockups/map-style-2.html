Map style, round 2 (#332)

Four independent blocks on one scene (a fragment of the real map arteriya). In each block pick a variant; the "side by side" checkbox shows all variants of the block in one row, the other blocks stay as selected. The owner’s choice (2026-10-21) on #332: take the D3 shading (gradients, soft shadows, border glow, highlights), no semi-3D landscape (no hillshade and no "lit hills"). Nothing changes in the game: the game code, maps:bake and the rules are untouched. A frame by address: #sh=1&tx=2&fg=2&rd=1&sc=100&gray=1&rm=1.

Roads: levels and states on a clean background (BL4)

Each row is a road variant (0 now, R1, R2, R3). Columns: your own internal road of levels 1, 2, 3; a buffer road (ends with different owners) of level 2; an enemy internal road in view; in the fog: the last seen level 2 and a never seen road (level 1 by DEC-17); construction 1→2; a supply packet and a one-off transfer. Grayscale and "reduced motion" apply here too. The fog veil (BL3) is not drawn here: you can see how the road style itself carries the state.

Small signs at natural size (1:1, independent of the map scale)

A check of the "soapiness" risk (ui-style 24.2): map signs on three backgrounds (dark forest, plains, snow: the worst case for a white outline). A meaningful symbol is not smaller than 20 px, a number not smaller than 10 px. Rows: BL1 shading strength (0, 1, 2). Look without zoom, at browser scale 100%.
