Now: the shipped province, the "+N / M" pill and the shipped places of the marks

1. Provinces that fit the current look

A province (DEC-23) has no city: no warehouse, no VP, no barracks or queue; capacity 3 (DEC-22). The shipped sign (C1) is a ring on a dark plate, and the owner finds the dark centre reads as a target. Every variant here is built from the parts the current map already uses: the BL1 body (the owner gradient, the paper outline, the light rim at the upper left, the soft shadow), the shipped capacity ring of segments (paper = your unit stands there, grey = room), the owner tint of the territory and the halftone of the terrain stamps. Every variant is lighter than a city (smaller, thinner outline, see-through or no body), has no dark centre and no second ring inside, keeps capacity 3 readable as three places outside the radius of a garrison token, and tells the side without colour: own a single outline, the enemy a second outline outside (the shipped inner dark ring of an enemy city is the "target" again, so it is not used), neutral grey and dashed. The last column puts the province next to a small city of the same owner to judge the weight. Click a row to pick it.

2. The "Defense" counter (DEC-30, #349)

DEC-30: a group in "Defense" gathers +1 bonus per orders.defense_ticks_per_bonus (60 ticks in mvp-1) up to a cap equal to its charged units; the bonus burns after a fight. Your own group carries defense_bonus and defense_cap in the view; for the enemy only the mode is public (DEC-30 item 5, AGENTS.md 4.4), never the number. Today the game shows a shield badge on the token and the text "+N / M" in a pill beside it (shapes.ts), plus the public intent shield at the upper left of the city. The owner wants a visual counter instead of the "N / M" text. Each variant is checked for: 0 of the cap (just switched on), partial, full, the cap growing (a charge arrives: 1 of 2 becomes 1 of 3), a large cap, no charged units (no bonus applies), the enemy view (mode only, also at PRESENCE). Readable without colour (filled vs hollow, long vs short) and at zoom 0.45 (the B1 icons never go below 16 px on screen). The samples use the layout picked above (section 3), on a medium city of the current look. Click a row to pick it.

3. The barracks icon and the Defense counter on the current city

Today the barracks is a disc with a little house on the left of the capacity ring (cityLayout.barracksAt), "Defense" is shown twice (the public intent shield at the upper left, intentView, and the shield badge with the pill on the token), the #439 cells sit above the ring, VP at the upper right, the C1 warehouse places under the ring and the caption under them. B1 (#337, #349) asked for one column of icons at the city. Each layout below places the barracks and the Defense counter picked in section 2 with every other mark of the current city, keeps the B1 icons at least 16 px on screen at any zoom, and shows one Defense sign instead of two (the sign at the city replaces the token badge and the upper-left intent shield; it is drawn from the same data: the group's mode where the group is seen, the public intent elsewhere). Under every sample the page checks the boxes of all the marks for overlaps (ring, cells, VP, barracks, Defense, warehouse, caption, token) and prints the result; at zoom 0.45 the 16 px floor of the icons and the 12-13 px captions make the marks large next to a small city, which the check shows honestly. Click a row to pick it.
