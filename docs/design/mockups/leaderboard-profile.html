1. The frame

Two screens that do not exist yet: the leaderboard (top 100 players) and the profile of a player (rating, record, the last 20 matches with a link to each replay). Both live in the frame of the start menu and of the lobby screens of #473: the real map as the postcard, the title bar with the player chip in the corner, the menu rail kept so the player always knows where they are. Three variants (D, W, S) differ only in how the content sits on the map; the rows, the history line and the states are the same pieces. Pick a screen, a state and an account above: the account (guest, calibration 2/5, ranked) changes the corner chip, the pinned own row and the profile of Profile of: Me; own rank beyond the top 100 pins the own row under the table; the other targets show another player's profile (ranked, calibrating, guest) and an unknown one. Click inside a frame: a nick opens its profile, Back returns, the rows of the rail switch the screen, Watch is only drawn.

4. What each piece needs from the server

Everything drawn comes from two read endpoints that exist in the server PR of #78 (no protocol change, no DEC, no new rule). Turn on needs from the server in the header to outline the pieces on the frame above. The client shows what the server says: it does not compute a rating, a rank, a result or whether a replay can be opened (AGENTS.md, section 7.1). The only derived numbers are display arithmetic: the win share (wins over matches) and the match length (ticks divided by 10).
