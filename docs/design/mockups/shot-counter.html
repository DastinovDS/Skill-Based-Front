Four ways to bring a counter back, plus the current look. The scene is the current map look (forest, own and enemy territory with the border glow, R1 lanes, C1 cities, U1 tokens, the AR1 plate, SH1 tracers), drawn by code. A real shot is a ■ square (the language of icons, ui-style 21: a charge and the head of a real tracer; the old plate used ●), a bonus shot a ◇ diamond; the opponent's signs have a double outline, so the side reads without color.

FPS: an estimate from the number of PixiJS objects (baked plates and glyph sprites, ui-style 24.4), not a measurement. "Data" names the fields of FightView (gow_protocol.view) the counter reads: last_round.shots, last_round.virtual (real = shots − virtual), last_round.losses, virtual_left, and the fight_round events with the same numbers. With combat.shots_per_round = 1 (mvp-1) a side makes at most one shot per round, so a per-round "number" is always 0 or 1: the round counters below show the kind of the shot (■, ◇ or none) and the number only matters for the fight totals (CT4) or for a ruleset with more shots per round.

Accessibility check (WCAG 2.1 AA, ui-style 22): the glyphs on the plate (#161b22) have 6.2:1 (yours, #5b9bff) and 4.4:1 (the opponent's, #e5484d), above the 3:1 for graphics; the white text 15.9:1, the dim text 7.1:1. In grayscale the two side colors differ by only 1.4:1, so the side is carried by the place (left and right on the plate, top and bottom in CT3, at its own group in CT2) and by the double outline of the opponent's glyphs and pills: checked with the grayscale switch. The counter text is 12 px (the caption minimum); "no shot" is a hollow ring, not a 2 px dot.
