1. The Supply view: nicer supply lines

The map mode on Tab or F2 (DEC-28, item 8; ui-style.md, section 19). Now is drawn from the same constants as the client (supplyView.ts, style.ts: SUPPLY_*), the cities are simplified (no capacity ring). What the owner wants nicer: the lines that show delivery. Every variant has to show seven things: the road level, the direction of the flow, the packets in transit, the front markers, cut-off cities, internal roads (supply goes only along them) against buffer roads (it stops there), and the volume. None needs a rule, a protocol or a server change: the scene is the own packets with their path, the own warehouses, me.fronts, starve_in and the level of every road, all in the snapshot today. The opponent stays territory and presence only (DEC-04, DEC-17). State is told by shape, number or pattern, not by colour alone: use the grayscale switch. The position is a made-up mid-game on the chosen map: two markers, a counter-flow on the spine (the automation sends a charge back, DEC-28 example 7), a one-off transfer, a city raided and cut off.

2. Terrain, and what it means for vision

Today the map shows three biomes (a baked background plus stamps), but nothing says what a terrain means: how well a group sees a neighbouring place depends on the terrain it stands on and the terrain it looks at (DEC-21, item 2), and since DEC-35 only own groups are sources (an empty own city sees nothing). The table is public (ruleset_public.vision.by_terrain) and the level of every place is already in the snapshot (detail: exact, range, presence, fog), so a variant only draws what the server sends; the client computes nothing. Three bars is the sign for a level in every variant: three exact, two range, one presence. The enemy token already tells it by its label (a number, a range, a question mark).

3. Rivers and lakes as purely visual borders

The owner's feedback of 2026-10-28: neighbouring places that have no road between them look connected. The 6 px strips of #428 were removed in #451. Here the answer is a landscape: no rule (nothing becomes impassable: there is simply no road there, and the picture now says why), no format change for A to C (the border between two neighbouring cells with no road between the two cities is found from nodes and roads that the client already has), and a river never meets a road (the check at the bottom counts crossings).
