Round 4 of the mockup for issue #431, after the owner's decision of 2026-10-29 on round 3 (rounds 1 to 3 are kept unchanged): planned orders: ODb Low relief is chosen, so every scene here uses ODb and there are no order variants; for units, cities and provinces none of the round 3 variants is chosen and the owner wants to see different variants again, with research of references and game-dev design guidance allowed. The research is in docs/design/research/431-r4-references.md (20 sources, the principles below come from it). Each new variant names the reference it comes from. Every variant avoids what the owner rejected: batteries; squares, blocks, cell grids; banners or flags; pawns or anything chess-like; war-game counters; medieval language (walls, towers, crenellations, bastions, gates); a recognizable setting or a borrowing from a known game. Three consistent sets (one unit, city and province family each) are in the header: A Transit #u=5&c=1&p=1, B Relief #u=3&c=2&p=2, C Settlement #u=1&c=3&p=3. The switch compare with Now draws the shipped look next to every new variant and adds a second scene with Now. Every frame is reproducible from the address. The mockup is SVG, not PixiJS: each look would be baked into one sprite per look in the client (SpriteCache, AGENTS.md 7.2, no filter per object); each card gives the bake and FPS cost as an estimate. What the player sees comes only from mechanics.md (DEC-21, DEC-22, DEC-26, DEC-29, DEC-31, DEC-35); no variant needs new data, a DEC, a protocol or a simulation change.

Now: the shipped units, cities and provinces, with ODb

1. Planned orders: ODb Low relief (chosen)

The owner chose ODb Low relief on 2026-10-29: OD with a short soft shadow, a faint even inner rim and no grain, with every round 2 and round 3 fix (one closed outline for the body and the head, the arc bulging toward the top of the screen, the head at the target rim or on the open target ring, the bonus beside the arc, the double-ring enemy tail). It is drawn in every scene of round 4; the row below is for reference only.

2. Units (group tokens)

Six new directions, each from a research principle: UN Cluster (Isotype: more marks, not bigger marks), UO Comet (transit line, repetition; the tail lies along the road), UP Phase (Bertin: value is the strongest ordered channel; the lit share of a disc), UQ Spark (preattentive length and line ends: a charged unit is a long ray, so the silhouette grows with the charge), UR Twin (transit interchange: two joined discs, units and charge), US Drop (a pointer that points at its place in a node and along the road on the way; the charge as now). Shared rules: the charge reads without colour (lit vs dark, long vs short, filled vs hollow); repeated marks are exact up to 7 (Spark 8, Comet 6) and a proportion above, the numeral is the truth; exact numbers only at EXACT (DEC-21); RANGE dashed with a "3-5" plate, PRESENCE a dashed "?", the memory ghost dashed with its age; the enemy has a second outline outside; the token never goes below 20 px on screen. The last two columns show a token on a road (Comet and Drop turn with the road, the others stay upright). Check at zoom 0.45 with grayscale on.

3. Cities (by kind and the capital)

Five new structures, none a polygon with corner marks: CL Interchange (joined stop rings, 1, 2, 3 by kind, the capital also a ring in the middle; transit ticks for the places), CM Terraces (a stepped plateau, 1 to 4 tiers, hachures for the places; hypsometric layer tints), CN Footprint (a built-up area with a stipple, more lobed by kind, an old-town core for the capital; landmark dots), CO Orbit (a core and a tilted orbit of places; two crossed orbits for the capital), CP Junction (the node as the junction of its real roads: a hub with an arm along each road; a double hub for the capital). In every variant the kind is a countable step or a distinct shape (graduated classes), a lit place mark = your unit stands there (capacity is public, DEC-22), and the fixed slots stay: the #439 cells above the sign, the B1 column (barracks #349 over Defense) on the left, VP at the upper right, the capital star badge at the upper left, the C1 warehouse places under the sign (own cities only, DEC-26) and the caption under them (#449). Own, enemy and neutral differ by the outline (single, double outside, dashed grey), not by the hue alone. No wall, tower, crenellation, bastion or gate.

4. Provinces

A province (DEC-23) has no city: no warehouse, no VP, no barracks or queue; capacity 3. Each new province is the matching city in its lightest form: PK Stop (CL), PL Contour (CM), PM Field (CN), PN Orbit (CO), PO Triad (the lightest, three dots). Every variant is clearly lighter than a city (lines, tints and small marks, no solid body), has no dark centre and no closed concentric rings and no triangle (no star with a ▽ token), puts its three place marks outside the radius of a garrison token and keeps the caption (12 px, lighter, hidden below zoom 0.7).

4b. Every unit on every province

Own garrison 3, 2 charged, on each province variant. The check for unintended figures: round 3 found that a ▽ token on a △ province makes a six-pointed star. Findings are in the cards and in #431. Click a row to pick the unit.
