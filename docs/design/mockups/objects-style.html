A mockup for issue #337. The scene: the same fragment of the real map arteriya as in #335, in the style chosen by the owner: BL1 shading "Restrained" with the border highlight of "Pronounced", the T2 texture "Thinning", the F1 fog "Haze", the R1 roads "Lanes". In the mockup you can change only what this round chooses: the four blocks below. Nothing beyond the fog rules: what the player sees is taken from mechanics.md, sections 3, 5 (DEC-30), 8 (DEC-26), 10 (DEC-04, DEC-21, DEC-31); where a variant needs new data, it is marked "protocol/DEC needed". The mockup is not PixiJS: it is drawn in SVG, the FPS prices in the issue are estimates from the ui-style 24.4 measurement.

1. Units (groups)

The shape, the number of units, the charge, your own and the opposing side not by color alone, the size at 60% scale. "Now" is the triangle ▽ from ui-style 9.5 (flat, as in the client); U1 has the same shape in the BL1 style. U2, U3 and U4 are bolder: they change the language of icons (ui-style 21), so each one can be accepted partially (for example, the U2 shape and the U1 caption). For groups in a city the icon stands in the center of the city (as now), for U3 (the banner) it is planted next to the city. "Defense": not drawn in this block, its look is decided by block 3 (which also has the "as now" variant: a shield on the corner of the icon). 2. Cities

A city by kind (small, medium, large), the capital, a province (DEC-23: no warehouse and no VP, capacity 3); the warehouse in squares (square = charge, ui-style 21), the warehouse level (DEC-26: warehouse capacity 1-6 at the start, +1 per level, at most 2 levels; there is no new field: warehouse_level, warehouse_cap are already on your own cities), ownership (your own, enemy, neutral: shape and outline, not only color). The warehouse of an enemy city is hidden (DEC-26, item 10): it is visible only together with exact vision and only the charges. In the scene and in the table: nodes 12 and 13 are shown as provinces (the MVP maps have none yet). A unit in a city is drawn by the chosen variant of block 1, the barracks and "Defense" by the chosen variant of block 3.

3. Barracks and "Defense" at a city

Barracks (DEC-26, DEC-31): your own is always visible; an enemy one only at a city adjacent to your own node (by the edges of the map graph), only a built one; the construction of an enemy city's barracks, the recruit queue and the warehouse are hidden; where it is unknown (None) and where there is none (False), the same thing is drawn: nothing. "Defense" (DEC-30): your own group shows the accumulated bonus and the cap "+1 / 3" (GroupView.defense_bonus and defense_cap, #317); for an enemy one only the mode is visible (from the PRESENCE level), not the number. The bonus is a virtual shot, so in the language of icons it is a rhombus ◇ (ui-style 21); "Defense" always belongs to a group standing in a node, so it can be drawn at a city. Several groups in a city: the icon shows the maximum over the groups (the tooltip clarifies; not a new field). The "Now" choice: a little house in a round icon to the left of the ring, a shield circle on the corner of the group icon.

4. Radial menu icons

A single set for the four menus (city: recruit, barracks, warehouse; group: split, defense, cancel, merge; stance: economy, balance, war; supply: transfer, front markers 1 and 2). Now: the city and supply menus use letter-like font symbols (+, ⌂, ▤, ⇢, ⚑), the group and stance menus have no icon (only a word), so the look depends on the font. The new sets are drawn here in SVG (our own authorship, no license needed, THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md does not change); Lucide from hud/icons.ts stays for the HUD (ui-style 8).
