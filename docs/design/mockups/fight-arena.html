Fight arena: variants of pace and bonus display

One and the same fight (examples 6, 9, 10, 15 from the table of section 4 "Fight over time" of mechanics.md) is played round by round on a map fragment. On the left, blue: you (attacking), on the right, red: the enemy (defending, the city is theirs). The switches below change the pace, the bonus display, the rules and the arena shape; the labels on the variants say what they need: no rules or protocol needs the protocol changes the rules, needs a DEC. This mockup changes nothing in the game. Sizes are in game world units (arena radius 24); the mockup scales to the width of the page.
