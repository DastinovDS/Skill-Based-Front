# Playtest 2026-10-30: four owner matches against the smart bots (BAL-02, #40)

The owner (a human) played four matches against the server bot levels of #574 with the bots of #618. This report
re-simulates the four command logs with `gow_sim` and measures CP, supply, upgrades, fights, the capital strikes and
the encirclements, then checks the owner's eight observations against the numbers. It changes no rule, no ruleset
number and no bot; the candidate changes at the end are **candidates for owner decisions, not decisions**.

- Map `crossroads` (49 nodes, 22 provinces; capitals Nexus (node 0, bot) and Core (node 48, human), 9 hops and
  624 packet ticks apart), ruleset `mvp-2` (hash `9d8cc4dd4b48572ad2be415c2fcc4d4c`), `SIM_VERSION` 25, develop at
  `0a6f888`. The human is side 1 in all four matches.
- Every log re-simulates to its last checkpoint (`gow_sim.replay.replay_match`); a scratch `MatchObserver` sampled
  the full state after every tick (CP, stance, nodes, groups, fights, packets, supplied territory) and kept every
  event and command result. `mise run stats` (`gow-bots stats`, #375) gave the command journal, the charge ledger
  (residual 0 in all eight player-matches) and the supply bottleneck counters. The replay files and the scripts are
  not in the repository (the owner has the replays).
- Definitions used below: a **front node** is a node with a neighbor owned by the opponent; a **pool** of a fight
  side is its charged units plus its virtual shots (terrain, plan bonus) over the whole fight, reinforcements
  included; a fight is **near-equal** when both sides have a charge and the pools differ by at most 1.
- Four matches and one human: every number is a description of these matches, not an estimate with an interval.

## Headline

1. **The bot lost the unit war, not the economy.** The bot lost 15-22 units in fights per match, the human 0-5
   (75 against 10 over the four matches). 51 of the bot's 75 losses came in 32 fights where it defended with a pool
   at least 2 below the attacker's: 1-2 unit garrisons left in the path of visible 3-unit charged groups. At the end
   the human had 15-32 units, the bot 2-14.
2. **CP is the scarce resource, charges are not.** Both sides burned charges for lack of room all match: the human
   43-56 of 103-144 produced (36-54%), the bot 66-115 of 115-170 (55-69%). CP was never at the cap for the bot and
   0.6-4.4% of the ticks for the human. The bot sat below 1 CP 39-60% of the ticks, the human 13-26%.
3. **Every capital strike came from a staging city next to the bot capital; the three that won came from one the bot
   never tried to retake.** The human
   took Middle Court (a large city of capacity 8, adjacent to Nexus) 490-990 ticks before each strike and joined
   2-4 groups from 2-4 directions into one capital fight: 11, 12 and 17 charged units (plus 2-8 plan shots) against
   4-5 charged defenders. The one strike that failed (match 2) is the one match where the bot retook Middle Court
   behind the strike.
4. **Supply is slow because the front is far, not because charges are short.** A charge from the capital took a
   median 17-27 s to arrive; the human's front-marker cities are 4-8 hops (24-53 s of level 1 road) from Core, and a
   marker city stood empty in 27-45% of the marker ticks although the capital held charges in every one of them.
5. **No rule contradiction found.** All 299 scheduled starvation ticks of the cut-off cities, the `cut_since` flag of
   every own city in every tick, and the 4 deaths of returning groups (DEC-24, G3) match `mechanics.md`. No bug
   issue was opened.

What the bot did and did not do:

- The capital warehouse was full (5-6) every time, but a garrison does not recharge during a fight (DEC-24, H1), so
  the defense was the 4-5 charges standing at contact plus the mountain shot. The human's other groups joined 12-103
  ticks after contact (a round every 20 ticks), while the defenders' shots were still cancelling the first arrivals.
- In matches 3 and 4 the bot had 15-16 units but 3-5 within one hop of its capital for the last minute, while 6-8
  charged human units stood in Middle Court in plain view (Nexus is in the mountains, Middle Court on plains: an exact
  view, DEC-21). Its commands in that minute went to far claims (White Spring, Glade, Fairgate, Lindenwood), stance
  flips, recruits it then cancelled (match 4: 5 recruits, 3 cancels), and it held 0.1-1.2 CP.
- It did not attack Middle Court in matches 1, 3 and 4 after losing it (0 orders into it). In match 2 it did, behind
  the strike, and the strike failed.

**Contrast with `smart_striker` (H-strike, log.md).** The striker gathered a fist "on a stage node within 2 hops",
was capped by the stack limit at 6-8 and split by provinces, was cut off in half of its matches and reached a fight
at the capital in 4 of 76 fist episodes; it planned 38% of its capital strikes and the plan bonus did not pay. The
human differed in five implementable ways:

1. **It won the unit war first** (above): by the strike it had 2x the bot's units, so the strike did not have to
   carry the corridor. The striker ignored VP and fought for a corridor with its CP.
2. **The stage was a large city adjacent to the capital**, not a node within 2 hops: one edge to walk, capacity 8 (the
   striker's 6-8 cap, but at distance 1), and a city with a warehouse, so the waiting units recharged.
3. **Several groups from several neighbors** arrived within 20-60 ticks into one fight: the stack limit caps a group,
   not a fight, and joins before the first rounds count in full (DEC-24, D1).
4. **Plans from 2-3 edges away**: the human's planned strike groups left from Freehold, Fork, Wallside (2-3 edges),
   so each carried 2-3 plan shots (DEC-29); from the adjacent stage a plan gives 1, which is what the striker had.
5. **It struck when the bot's army was elsewhere**: 3-5 bot units within one hop against 6-16 in the match.

## Encirclement (observation 8)

Cut-off episodes (an own city outside the supplied territory, DEC-19): 15, 28, 19 and 15 per match; the long ones
(at least 100 ticks or with a starvation) are listed by the scratch scripts. Checks against `mechanics.md`:

- `cut_since` is set on an own city exactly in the ticks when it is outside the supplied territory
  (`gow_sim.systems.capacity.supplied_territory`), in every tick of the four matches: 0 mismatches.
- Every scheduled consumption tick `cut_since + 100 + k * 100` (299 in all): the spent charges equal
  `min(available, ceil(U / 3))` with `U` the owner's standing units, and nothing is spent at `U = 0`: 0 mismatches.
- The 4 G3 deaths match item G3 (above).

What matches the rules but feels odd, and why:

1. **One capture cuts a whole region off.** In match 3 the human's capture of White Spring (tick 4903) cut six bot
   cities off for 147 s (Watchtower, North Outpost, Millpond, Shale, Newstead, Willowbed). Supply runs only over own
   nodes (section 9, SIM-07), so a neutral or enemy province in a chain of cities breaks it.
2. **A cut-off pocket barely starves.** Those six cities lost 8 charges in 147 s: starvation spends
   `ceil(U / 3)` charges per 10 s after a 10 s grace (DEC-19, item 3), and most of the pocket had no standing units
   (`U = 0`, nothing spent). In match 1, Lodge stayed cut off for 1061 ticks without a single consumption.
3. **A group dies at "home" without being outnumbered.** In match 2 the human's 4-unit group, discharged by the failed
   strike, bounced back to Middle Court, which the bot had retaken 21 ticks earlier with 3 charged units: two died
   to shots and the other two by G3 (a returning group that does not take its former node dies, DEC-24, A2 and G3).
   The rule removes the second bounce on purpose; with the origin lost there is no way home.

No contradiction with `mechanics.md` was found, so no bug issue was opened.

## Bot weaknesses found (smart bots of #618)

1. **Thin garrisons in front of visible stacks**: 51 of 75 units lost defending with a pool 2+ below the attacker; no
   evacuation or concentration when a 3-unit charged group stands next door.
2. **No answer to a stage next to the capital**: 0 orders into a lost adjacent large city in 3 of 4 matches, 3-5 units
   within one hop of the capital while 15-16 exist; the one counter-attack (match 2) beat the strike.
3. **Stance**: 14-27 changes a match, 45-73% of the ticks in Balance while 55-69% of its charges burned: 6-13 CP a
   match thrown away.
4. **CP on 1-unit claims**: in matches 1-2, 70 orders into empty human nodes (recaptures back and forth); the bot was below 1 CP
   39-60% of the ticks, so plans (#618) and recruits were unaffordable when needed.
5. **No forward barracks that survives**: 3 barracks, 2 lost within 20 s, 3 recruits; the human's one central
   barracks gave 14-15.

## Candidate changes the data supports (for owner decisions, not decisions)

Bots (no rule change):

- B1. A capital-threat response: when the visible enemy charged units within 1-2 hops of the own capital exceed the
  capital's charged garrison plus the mountain shot, pull units into the capital and attack the stage while it forms
  (match 2 shows that retaking the stage defeats the strike).
- B2. Do not leave a garrison whose pool is 2+ below a visible adjacent enemy group: leave early (before contact,
  DEC-37 catches only 3 ticks) or merge it into a neighbor.
- B3. Stay in War while charges burn (`charges_overflow` in the last minute); switch to Balance only when the front
  is short of charges.
- B4. A forward barracks in a central large city held by the main army (Crossroads on this map), early (the human:
  tick 1100).

Ruleset numbers (each needs a tournament on GitHub runners before a decision):

- R1. More CP against fewer charges: both sides burn 36-69% of the charges while CP is the binding constraint. For
  example `cp.per_period` 100 -> 120, or the War share `economy.stance_to_charges_pct.war` 40 -> 30 (BAL-02 iteration 3
  raised it from 25; the bots then had no use for charges, and neither did the human here).
- R2. Faster supply to a far front: `supply.base_speed_pct` 100 -> 150 (the level 2 speed for every road), or a
  cheaper road level; the human's marker cities are 24-53 s away at level 1.

Rules (each would be a new DEC):

- D1. Recharge in a province: a province adjacent to an own city with a warehouse recharges standing units from it
  (or provinces get a warehouse of 1). Observation 5 is a direct consequence of DEC-23.
- D2. The cost of a retreat: with `shots_per_round = 1` the first rounds cancel 1:1, so an outgunned group leaves
  with all its units. Options: a leaving group gets the surplus shots of the round it leaves in, or
  `combat.shots_per_round = 2` (an alternative already listed in DEC-24).
- D3. G3 for a lost origin: let a returning group whose origin fell continue to the nearest own neighbor instead of
  dying (DEC-24, A2 chose death on purpose; a question, not a recommendation).
