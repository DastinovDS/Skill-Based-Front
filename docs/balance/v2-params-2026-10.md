# Economy v2: parameter experiments (#659, 2026-10-31)

Question of the owner's playtest: under `mvp-3` CP is in surplus, charges burn at full factories, a capital is hard
to take. Nothing here changes `rulesets/mvp-3.toml`; the candidates are `docs/balance/candidates/mvp-3-*.toml`
(a full copy of `mvp-3.toml` with the changed keys named in the header). The owner chooses (`Refs #659`).

## Method

- Field: 15 smart bots (5 characters x easy, normal, hard) + `smart_hoarder`; every ordered pair, both sides; `mvp-3`
  with the one change per variant listed below.
- Old field = bots of `develop` before #663 (`SIM_VERSION` 29, 3 pool maps). New field = after #663 (PR #669) with the
  4 pool maps (`narrows` added).
- Groups A and B: 10 seeds per map and pair on GitHub runners (7680 matches). Group C and the first final pair: 4
  seeds (3072 matches), run locally; the `mvp-3` row of those tables is the same 4 seeds taken out of the 10-seed run.
  Confirmation on the new field: 20 seeds (20480 matches per ruleset).
- Metrics from the tournament rows (`tournament.jsonl`): CP spent = (moves x `orders.cost.move` + planned moves x
  `planned_move` + recruits x `recruit`) over the income (`cp.start` + `cp.per_period` per `period_ticks`), no
  allowance for the cap burn, as in #648; burned, captures, capital wins, ticks as in the rows; "win sd" = standard
  deviation of the 16 bots' win rates (draws count 0.5, mirror matches left out); "charges made" = production per
  player and match; burned = share of start + production. The runs are `mise run tournament:gh` (A, B, new field) and
  `mise run tournament` (C, first F pair).

## Group A: CP (hypothesis: bots are limited by their tempo, so costs alone will not bind; income must fall)

| variant | change vs `mvp-3` | CP spent | captures | capital wins | win sd | bots over 0.80 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mvp-3` | | 0.61 | 41.7 | 8.6% | 0.264 | 3 |
| A1 | `cp.period_ticks` 70 | 0.72 | 40.9 | 5.7% | 0.264 | 2 |
| A2 | `orders.cost.move` and `planned_move` 150 | 0.67 | 39.5 | 5.0% | 0.269 | 3 |
| A3 | `orders.cost.recruit` 200 | 0.67 | 41.0 | 8.5% | 0.258 | 2 |
| A4 | period 60, move, planned, recruit 130 | 0.70 | 38.8 | 6.0% | 0.269 | 3 |
| A5 | `cp.period_ticks` 100 | 0.81 | 38.1 | 5.7% | 0.259 | 2 |
| A6 | move, planned 200, recruit 150 | 0.75 | 37.0 | 3.8% | 0.260 | 2 |
| A7 | period 80, move, planned, recruit 150 | 0.83 | 35.7 | 4.4% | 0.255 | 2 |

Result: prices barely move the share (the bots queue fewer commands before they run dry); halving the income does
(A5 0.81 for -9% captures). The 0.85 target needs an income below half (period 100 and a little more, or A7), and
every CP squeeze costs some tempo: captures -9 to -14%, capital wins down a third to a half.

## Group B: factories and lines (hypothesis: burning is overproduction, not too little line throughput)

| variant | change vs `mvp-3` | burned | charges made | captures | capital wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `mvp-3` | | 0.40 | 332 | 41.7 | 8.6% |
| B1 | `factory.capital_period_ticks` 70, `built_period_ticks` 140 | 0.28 | 242 | 38.2 | 4.8% |
| B2 | `warehouse.start_by_kind.capital` 10 | 0.39 | 335 | 41.9 | 7.9% |
| B3 | `factory.max` 2, `factory.cost` 5 | 0.32 | 271 | 40.5 | 6.5% |
| B4 | `supply.dispatch_ticks` 5 (twice the line throughput) | 0.40 | 334 | 41.7 | 8.3% |

Result: a bigger capital warehouse and faster lines change nothing (the bots draw too few lines, not too slow
ones); only less production lowers the burn, and the win spread stays as it was. Under 20% needs production near
half (capital 100 / built 200 ticks).

## Recommendation: `balanced` (`docs/balance/candidates/mvp-3-balanced.toml`)

| key | `mvp-3` | `balanced` |
|---|---|---|
| `cp.period_ticks` | 50 | 100 (CP income 1 per 10 s) |
| `factory.capital_period_ticks` | 50 | 100 |
| `factory.built_period_ticks` | 100 | 200 |
| `capture.charge_cost` | 1 | 0 |

In play: CP is a real budget (about 90% spent by the bots, one order per 10 s of income on top of the start stock);
a factory makes half as many charges, so a charge is something to carry to the front and not a stock that burns at
the warehouse (burn 0.36 to 0.22); a capture no longer costs a charge, so the front moves, the capital falls in
about one match in eight (7.7% to 12.4%) and matches end about 250 ticks (25 s) earlier. The hold-and-wait character
(the turtle) loses its edge (0.79 to 0.46); the attacking and the reactive ones gain.

Not met: the burn target of 20% (0.22; `conservative` reaches 0.20 but there the capital almost never falls, 2.6%).
Open choice for the owner: `capture.charge_cost` 0 deletes the DEC-38 capture price (a rule change, `SIM_VERSION` +1
and mechanics.md), while the other three keys are numbers only. `conservative` keeps DEC-38 and has the flattest field
(sd 0.150) but a capital win only one match in 40.

Caveats: bots only (a human plays at another tempo, so the CP share is a bot figure); groups C and the first final pair have 4 seeds (win-rate intervals about +-0.03); production is halved, so the
start stock matters more (not tuned here); the hash of a candidate is its own, no golden replay uses it.
